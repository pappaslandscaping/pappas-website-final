À La Carte Services

Pick and choose exactly what you need

Lawn Maintenance

Weekly mowing, edging, and trimming to keep your lawn looking sharp. We're talking crisp lines, perfectly striped grass, and that "just mowed" smell every week.

Lawn Installations

Starting fresh? We'll install a brand new lawn that'll have your neighbors wondering if you laid down carpet. Whether it's sod or seed, we'll get you set up right.

Shrub & Hedge Trimming

Nobody wants overgrown bushes blocking their windows. We'll keep your shrubs and hedges neat, tidy, and actually adding to your curb appeal instead of hiding it.

Spring Cleanups

Winter is rough on lawns. We'll clear out all the dead leaves, sticks, and winter debris so your yard is ready to actually look good when the weather finally cooperates.

Fall Cleanups

Running from late October through November, we'll handle all those fallen leaves and get your property buttoned up before winter hits. No more weekend leaf-raking marathons.

Mulching

Fresh mulch makes everything look better—it's like the finishing touch that pulls your whole landscape together. Plus it keeps weeds down and your plants happy.

Aeration

Your lawn needs to breathe. We'll poke holes in that compacted soil so water, air, and nutrients can actually reach the roots. Think of it as a spa day for your grass.

Fertilization

Custom fertilization plans to give your lawn exactly what it needs, when it needs it. We're talking thick, green, healthy grass that doesn't quit.

Weed Control

Dandelions, crabgrass, and all those other uninvited guests? We'll show them the door. Comprehensive weed control to keep your lawn looking pristine.

Snow Removal

Cleveland winters are no joke. We'll be out there clearing your driveway and walkways before you even finish your morning coffee. Reliable, fast, and no 3am wake-up calls for you.
