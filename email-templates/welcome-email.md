Since you just signed up, here's a quick spring checklist for Cleveland-area lawns. Knock out a few of these and your yard will thank you all summer:

**This week or next:**
- Get pre-emergent down before crabgrass germinates (when forsythia is blooming, you've got about a week)
- Walk your property and note any winter damage to fix
- Check that sprinkler heads weren't damaged by snow plows

**Late April / Early May:**
- First mow of the season (set your mower to 3 to 3.5 inches, no shorter)
- Spring cleanup: clear debris, edge beds, trim back perennials
- Apply your first round of fertilizer once grass is actively growing

**Sometime in May:**
- Mulch any beds that need a refresh (2 to 3 inches deep, no mulch volcanoes around tree trunks)
- Aerate if your soil feels compacted

That's enough to keep you busy. We'll be back next time the season changes with what to focus on then.

## Setup Instructions for Netlify Forms

Netlify Forms can send a notification email when someone signs up, but it doesn't send an auto-reply to the signer by default. You have two options:

### Option A: Quick and free (manual)
Set up Netlify to send YOU a notification when someone signs up, and just reply manually with the welcome email above. Works fine when signups are low (under 5 a week).

To set up: Netlify dashboard → Site → Forms → Form notifications → Add notification → Email. Add hello@pappaslandscaping.com.

### Option B: Automated welcome email (recommended once signups grow)
Connect Netlify Forms to a tool like Mailchimp, ConvertKit, or Brevo (formerly Sendinblue, free tier available). These tools auto-send the welcome email, let you build a subscriber list, and let you send seasonal campaigns.

Recommended for your scale: Brevo (free for up to 300 emails/day) or Mailchimp (free for up to 500 contacts).

When you're ready to set this up, I can walk you through the integration.
