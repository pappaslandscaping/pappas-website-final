# Review Request Templates

Use these templates to ask happy customers for Google reviews. Goal: double or triple your review count over the next 60 days.

## Why this matters

Each Google review is a ranking signal. More 4-5 star reviews = higher placement in the local map pack = more leads. It also boosts conversion when potential customers are comparing you to competitors. People trust businesses with 50+ reviews way more than businesses with 5.

## Your direct review link

To make it as easy as possible for customers, send them a direct link to leave a review:

1. Go to business.google.com
2. Sign in to Pappas & Co. Landscaping
3. On the home dashboard, find the "Get more reviews" card
4. Click "Share review form" to copy your direct link
5. Use that link in every template below (replace [REVIEW_LINK] with your actual link)

The link will look something like: https://g.page/r/CXXXXXXXX/review

**When to send:** A few days after a successful job, or after the first mow of a new season for ongoing customers. Texts get the highest response rate.

## Best Practices

**Do:**
- Send within a few days of a job for best results
- Personalize with the customer's name (never "Hey valued customer")
- Make it short and clear what you want
- Always include the direct link, never make them search
- Send to customers you KNOW are happy
- Reply to every review you get, good or bad, within 24 hours

**Don't:**
- Offer discounts or anything in exchange for reviews (against Google's rules and can get your profile penalized)
- Send to customers who have ever complained
- Send to multiple customers from the same IP at the same time (looks like spam to Google)
- Get discouraged if response rate is 20-30%, that's totally normal

---

## Suggested Cadence

**Going forward:** Pick 5 happy customers per week and send a request. That's 20 a month. Even at a 25% response rate, that's 5 new reviews per month, 60 per year.

**Right now (one-time push):** Make a list of your 30 best customers and send all 30 a review request over 2-3 days. Even 8-10 new reviews will significantly boost your local SEO.

---

## What to Do When You Get a Negative Review

1. Don't panic
2. Reply within 24 hours, publicly
3. Acknowledge the issue, apologize for the experience, take it offline ("I'd love to make this right. Please call me directly at (440) 886-7318 so we can resolve this." )
4. Never argue or get defensive in the public reply
5. Try to genuinely fix the problem so the customer updates their review

A handful of negative reviews mixed in with mostly positive ones actually makes your business look MORE trustworthy, not less. People assume an all-5-star profile is fake.
