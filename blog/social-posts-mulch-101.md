### Facebook Post 2 (The Common Mistake Hook)

Please stop making mulch volcanoes.

You know the ones: mulch piled up against the trunk of a tree like a little mountain. It looks tidy, but it's actually one of the worst things you can do for your tree. Traps moisture against the bark, encourages rot, invites insects.

The rule: think donut, not volcano. Keep mulch 2-3 inches away from the trunk so the tree can breathe.

### Facebook Post 3 (The Practical Answer)

"How much mulch do I actually need?"

This comes up every single spring. Here's the quick answer: one cubic yard covers about 100 square feet at 3 inches deep. For a typical Cleveland home with a few flower beds and some trees, you're probably looking at 3 to 5 cubic yards.

And 2 to 3 inches deep is the sweet spot. Less than that and it won't stop weeds. More than 4 inches can actually suffocate your plant roots.

### LinkedIn Post 2 (Tim, first person: The Mulch Volcano Story)

The #1 landscaping mistake I see in Cleveland yards isn't mulch type or timing. It's the mulch volcano.

You've seen them. That tidy little mound of mulch piled up around a tree trunk. Looks neat. It's slowly killing the tree.

Here's what's actually happening: the piled mulch traps moisture against the bark, keeps the trunk from breathing, invites insects and disease, and encourages roots to grow upward into the mulch instead of deep into the ground. I've watched mature, healthy trees decline over a few seasons because of it.

The fix takes about 10 seconds per tree. Pull the mulch back so it's 2-3 inches away from the trunk. Think donut shape, not volcano. Your trees will thank you.

## Posting Tips

- **Facebook:** Best times for local engagement are weekday mornings (7-9 AM) and weekday evenings (7-9 PM). Wednesday and Thursday tend to perform best.
- **Instagram:** Post during lunch (11 AM-1 PM) or evening (7-9 PM). Use all hashtags, they help local reach.
- **LinkedIn:** Tuesday through Thursday, 8-10 AM. Avoid weekends.
- **Spread them out:** Don't post all three variations on the same platform in the same week. Space them 5-7 days apart.
- **Pair with a photo:** For Facebook and Instagram, a before/after mulch photo from a recent job will significantly boost engagement.
- **Engage with comments:** Reply to every comment within the first hour if possible. This boosts reach on all three platforms.
