### LinkedIn Post 2 (The Expertise Angle)

A question we get every spring: "Why does my crabgrass keep getting worse every year?"

The answer is math, not luck. A single crabgrass plant drops up to 150,000 seeds before frost kills it. Those seeds stay viable in your soil for 3-5 years. Every year you don't control it, you're building up an inventory of seeds waiting for their moment.

The good news: breaking that cycle is straightforward. It takes one well-timed pre-emergent application, a consistent fertilization schedule, and mowing high enough to shade the soil.

## Posting Tips

- **Facebook:** Best times for local engagement are weekday mornings (7-9 AM) and weekday evenings (7-9 PM). Wednesday and Thursday tend to perform best.
- **Instagram:** Post during lunch (11 AM-1 PM) or evening (7-9 PM). Use all hashtags, they help local reach.
- **LinkedIn:** Tuesday through Thursday, 8-10 AM. Avoid weekends.
- **Spread them out:** Don't post all three variations on the same platform in the same week. Space them 5-7 days apart.
- **Engage with comments:** Reply to every comment within the first hour if possible. This boosts reach on all three platforms.
