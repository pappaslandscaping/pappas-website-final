How We Got Here

Back in 2004, Tim started working for Grounds by Coffey, learning the ropes of what makes a lawn go from "meh" to magnificent. Twenty years later, he's still obsessed with the perfect cut, the ideal stripe pattern, and lawns that make people actually want to mow (okay, maybe that's a stretch).

After years of perfecting his craft, Tim decided it was time to do his own thing—and Pappas & Co. Landscaping was born. The mission? Simple: bring that same meticulous, no-shortcuts approach to Cleveland-area lawns, but with a family touch that the big guys just can't match.

Here's the thing: we're not a faceless lawn service. Tim's the guy you'll see on your property. Theresa (Tim's wife) is running the behind-the-scenes magic—scheduling, customer care, making sure everything runs like clockwork. And when he's home from college, their son Aidan jumps in to help keep the family tradition alive.

We've been serving Cleveland, Lakewood, Bay Village, Brook Park, and beyond for years now. Same promise, same standards, same obsession with making your outdoor space look incredible—whether it's the peak of summer or the dead of winter.

What We're All About

Look, our mission isn't complicated: we want your lawn to be the one that makes your neighbors wonder if they should step up their game. We're talking about the kind of lawn that looks great for your backyard BBQs, impresses your in-laws, and maybe even earns you the unofficial "Best Lawn on the Block" title.
