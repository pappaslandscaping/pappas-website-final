# Photo Plan for Pappas & Co. Website

## Why this matters

Your site uses illustrations of you, Tim, Aidan, and Chris (which is charming and on-brand), but adding real photos of your work will significantly improve conversion. People hire landscapers based on visual proof.

Photos also boost SEO when properly tagged, and they're essential for your Google Business Profile (the #1 conversion factor there).

## Equipment

You don't need anything fancy. Your iPhone is fine. Just shoot in landscape mode, in good light (early morning or late afternoon look best), and try to get a clean, uncluttered background.

## Shot List (priority order)

### Tier 1: Take these ASAP (biggest impact)

1. **Before/After mowing pairs** — Take 3-4 properties where you can clearly see the transformation. Same angle, before and after.
2. **A perfectly edged sidewalk or driveway** — Tim's signature crisp lines. Closeup shot.
3. **A freshly mulched flower bed** — Wide angle showing the whole bed, plus a close-up of the texture.
4. **A spring or fall cleanup before/after** — Pile of leaves and debris vs clean lawn.
5. **The team in action** — Tim or Chris actually working (mowing, edging, mulching, etc.). Action shots, not posed.
6. **Snow removal in action** — Truck or shovel clearing a driveway. Save these from this past winter or take during the next snow.

### Tier 2: Nice to have

7. **Your truck or trailer with the logo visible** — Brand recognition.
8. **Equipment lineup** — Mowers, trimmers, blowers neatly arranged.
9. **A "happy customer" testimonial-style photo** — A homeowner standing in their freshly-cared-for yard (with permission).
10. **Close-ups of healthy grass, fresh fertilizer pellets, mulch texture** — Visual texture for blog posts.

### Tier 3: Eventually

11. **Drone shots of larger properties** — If you have a friend with a drone, this looks really professional.
12. **Seasonal property tours** — Same property in spring, summer, fall, winter.

## Where to use them

Once you have photos, here's where they'll go:

1. **Google Business Profile** — Upload at least 10-15 to start. Add a few new ones every month. This is the highest-ROI use.
2. **Services page** — Replace or supplement the current generic look with real photos of each service.
3. **Homepage hero** — Consider replacing the current hero with a real lawn photo (we'd need to test this).
4. **Reviews page** — Pair customer testimonials with photos of their actual properties.
5. **Blog posts** — Each new post can include a relevant photo.
6. **Social media** — Photos get 3-5x more engagement than text-only posts.

## Naming convention (for SEO)

When saving photos, use descriptive names with keywords:

- ✅ Good: `lakewood-spring-cleanup-before-after.jpg`
- ✅ Good: `bay-village-mulched-flower-bed.jpg`
- ✅ Good: `tim-pappas-edging-driveway-cleveland.jpg`
- ❌ Bad: `IMG_4273.jpg`
- ❌ Bad: `photo1.jpg`

Each filename should answer: what's in the photo + what suburb (if relevant). Google reads filenames and image alt text for SEO.

- **Week 2:** Upload to Google Business Profile, integrate into services page
- **Ongoing:** Add 2-3 new photos per week as you do jobs

You don't need to do this all at once. Even adding 5 great photos this week would be a meaningful upgrade.
